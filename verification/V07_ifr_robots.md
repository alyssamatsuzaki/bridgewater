**Verification date:** 2026-07-31

## Tooling note (read before the table)

`ifr.org` and essentially every other external domain (Wikipedia, therobotreport.com, businesswire.com, etc.) returned **HTTP 403** to every `WebFetch` call in this session, and raw `curl` from Bash was rejected at the egress-proxy level with `403` on `CONNECT` (confirmed via `$HTTPS_PROXY/__agentproxy/status` — `ifr.org:443`, `en.wikipedia.org:443`, `www.therobotreport.com:443` all logged as `connect_rejected`). This is a session/org network-policy block, not evidence about the sources themselves. I could not open a single ifr.org page or PDF directly.

All figures below therefore come from `WebSearch` result snippets, which do quote press-release text (including several verbatim numeric strings I searched for specifically, e.g. `"373,240"`, `"541,302"`, `"542,076"`, `"517,385"`) and from secondary syndication of the same IFR press releases (BusinessWire reprints, trade press: The Robot Report, Robotics 24/7, Manufacturing AUTOMATION, heise online, statzon, SCIO). Where a figure is corroborated only by such secondary reprints rather than a fetched ifr.org original, I've labeled the channel accordingly and marked status **APPROXIMATE** rather than **CONFIRMED**. The `WebSearch` tool's per-session call budget was also exhausted (200/200) partway through this task, which limited how many follow-up precision-checking queries I could run (noted per row below).

Given these constraints, treat rows without a directly-fetched ifr.org primary as provisionally confirmed, pending a re-run with working ifr.org access (or the paywalled World Robotics report itself) to nail down exact unit-level figures.

## Question-by-question answers

**1. 2023 (WR2024 edition):** CONFIRMED. China 276,288 / World 541,302 → 51.0%, and IFR's own press release states "51%" in prose. Draft is correct as-is.

**2. 2024 (WR2025 edition):** APPROXIMATE, with the precision flag confirmed. China 295,000 (rounded) / World 542,076 (precise) → 54.4% by division, but IFR's own stated share is "54%." The unit-count precision mismatch the task asked me to check is real: it is unusual for the aggregate world figure in a "preliminary" first-print release to be more precise (542,076) than the single-country headline figure (295,000, a round number). I could not resolve the true precise China count with tools available this session (World Robotics 2025 report is paywalled, ifr.org blocked).

**3. 2015:** Covered by **World Robotics 2016** (published ~2016; industrial robots report). China ~68,600 units (+20% YoY), world ~254,000 units — both explicitly stated by IFR as rounded ("around ___"). Computed share = 27.0%, matching the draft's plotted 27.0% almost exactly. Marked APPROXIMATE because I could not retrieve unit-level precision, but the rounded figures reproduce the draft's number closely enough to have real confidence in it.

**4. 2019:** Covered by **World Robotics 2020** (press release 2020-09-24). China ~140,500 (−9%), world 373,240 (exact). Computed share 37.6%, matching draft. CONFIRMED.

**5. 2020:** Covered by **World Robotics 2021**. China 168,377 (+20%, exact), world ~384,000 (rounded in the text I could retrieve). Computed share 43.8%, vs. draft's 43.9% — a 0.1-point gap I could not fully close given a rounded world denominator and exhausted search budget. Flagged APPROXIMATE/minor discrepancy for author re-check, not CONTRADICTED, since it is well within the uncertainty introduced by a to-the-nearest-1,000 world figure.

**6. 2021:** Covered by **World Robotics 2022**. China 268,195 (+51%), world 517,385 (+31%, all-time high at the time) — both exact. Computed share 51.8%, matching draft exactly. CONFIRMED.

**7. 2022:** Covered by **World Robotics 2023** (press release 2023-09-26). China 290,258 (+5%), world 553,052 (+5%) — both exact. Computed share 52.5%, matching draft. CONFIRMED. (See Q8 for the subsequent small revision, which does not change the rounded share.)

**8. Revision practice:** Confirmed IFR does revise prior-year figures in later editions, at least for 2022: the World Robotics 2024 press release's historical comparison line ("...second highest level ever recorded (2022: 290,144 units)" and "...552,946 units installed in 2022") shows the 2022 China figure revised down from 290,258 → 290,144 (−114 units, ≈−0.04%) and the 2022 world figure revised down from 553,052 → 552,946 (−106 units, ≈−0.02%) between the WR2023 and WR2024 editions. Recomputing the 2022 share with the revised figures gives 52.47% vs. the original 52.48% — both round to **52.5%**, so this particular revision does **not** move any plotted share point by more than 0.5 points (it moves it by roughly 0.01 points). I could not verify, with tools available this session (WebSearch budget exhausted, WebFetch blocked throughout), whether IFR similarly revised the 2015, 2019, 2020, or 2021 figures in subsequent editions — I found no evidence either way for those years and could not chase it further. This is a residual gap: a full re-check would need to compare each year's figure as printed in its "home" edition against how the same year is cited in the next 1-2 editions' historical-comparison tables, which requires reaching ifr.org or the syndicated PDFs directly.

**9. Publication cadence:** CONFIRMED, annual, consistently around September, with no gap evidence found. Observed press-release dates: World Robotics 2019 (2019-09-18), World Robotics 2020 (2020-09-24), World Robotics 2021 (~Sept/Oct 2021), World Robotics 2022 (Sept 2022), World Robotics 2023 (2023-09-26), World Robotics 2024 (2024-09-24), World Robotics 2025 (2025-09-25), with World Robotics 2026 already reported as scheduled for 2026-09-24. IFR's "About World Robotics" materials describe the underlying database as going back to 1993. I did not individually confirm every year back to 1993, but found no evidence of any missed year in the range examined (2015–2026 scheduled).

- **WebFetch was non-functional for essentially the entire session**: every attempted fetch (ifr.org pages and PDFs, LinkedIn, Wikipedia, The Robot Report, heise.de, robot-forum.com) returned "The server returned HTTP 403 Forbidden," and raw `curl` via Bash was independently rejected by the egress proxy with logged `connect_rejected / 403` entries for `ifr.org`, `en.wikipedia.org`, and `www.therobotreport.com`. - **WebSearch's per-session budget (200 calls) was exhausted** before I could run every planned precision-check query (particularly: precise non-rounded 2020 world total, and a systematic revision-check sweep for 2015/2019/2020/2021 across later editions).
- Because of the above, every figure in this file is sourced from **WebSearch result snippets** rather than a directly-fetched primary document. The snippets do appear to quote real press-release/article text (confirmed by searching for exact numeric strings and getting consistent hits across independent outlets), but I was not able to open a single ifr.org page myself to eyeball it directly, as instructed. **Recommend a follow-up pass once ifr.org access is restored**, specifically to: (a) directly confirm the precise 2020 world total, (b) directly confirm the precise (non-rounded) 2024 China figure, and (c) check whether 2015/2019/2020/2021 figures were revised in later editions.
